How to use the random wheel

Three steps and you’re done. No sign-up, no downloads.

Add your options

Type one option per line or pick a quick template. Great for games, giveaways, classroom picks or everyday decisions.

2

Spin the wheel

Tap the wheel, the “Spin” button or your Space bar, and watch it slow down until it lands on a random pick.

3

Celebrate & repeat

Frequently asked questions

Is the wheel really random?

Yes. The winner is chosen with your browser’s cryptographically secure random generator before the animation starts, so every option has exactly the same chance.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It’s completely free and works right in your browser. No sign-up, no downloads.

Where are my options saved?

Only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing you type is uploaded to a server, and you can clear it anytime with “Clear all”.

Can I use it for giveaways and raffles?

Yes. After each spin, choose “Remove winner” so the same name can’t win twice. If you run an official giveaway, make sure it follows the rules where you live.

How many options can I add?

Up to 100 options of up to 60 characters each. The slices and text resize automatically.
